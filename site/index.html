About BlueCoin

Simple token design. Public infrastructure.

BlueCoin (BLUEX) is a community-driven digital token built on TON for fast, low-cost and secure transfers. The contract is finalized: the metadata is locked, minting is disabled and ownership is revoked.

The project is currently completing ecosystem verification and expanding its public infrastructure. Detailed allocation and utility plans will be published only after they are finalized.
